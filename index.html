What it does

Finished work, not just answers

Describe the outcome in plain language. Cera plans the steps, carries them out in your desktop applications, and hands back the result with the evidence that it is done.

Operates your desktop

Opens applications, fills in forms, clicks through dialogs and reads the screen, the way a colleague at the keyboard would, then checks that each step actually happened.

Produces deliverables

Specialised digital workers produce saved workbooks, sourced reports and completed operations, each with a validation record you can audit.

Plans and schedules

Builds and updates project schedules in Primavera P6 Professional and Microsoft Project, including custom VBA macros and ribbon buttons.

Joins meetings

Attends Microsoft Teams meetings on request, under a participation policy you choose.

Browses on your behalf

Uses its own isolated browser, kept separate from yours, so your personal sessions are never touched.

Answers offline

An optional offline library of medical, emergency and how-to references, answered by the local model with no internet connection.

How it works

From request to verified result

Ask

Describe what you need in the chat window, in your own words.

Plan

Cera turns the request into a concrete plan and classifies how much risk each step carries.

Approve

Anything sensitive waits for your confirmation, and that approval covers that exact plan only.

Verify

Cera checks the outcome on screen. If it cannot confirm it, it says so instead of claiming success.

Governance

Built to be trusted with a real desktop

Safety in Cera is enforced by the software itself, not left to the model's judgement. The rules below hold no matter what the agent is asked to do.

  • One controlled path to your desktopEvery action goes through a single checkpoint. Nothing reaches your applications any other way.
  • An off switch that always worksA global kill switch stops all activity at once. Only a person verified with Windows Hello can turn it back on.
  • Approvals tied to a real personSensitive plans need a single-use approval for that exact plan, recorded against a verified approver.
  • Autonomy you controlThe agent starts at the lowest autonomy level. Anything self-directed has to be permitted first.
  • Private by designThe model runs on your own hardware, so your documents and conversations stay on your machine.
